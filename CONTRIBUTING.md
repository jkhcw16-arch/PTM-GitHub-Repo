# Contributing to Pairwise Temporal Memory

PTM is currently developed under an all-rights-reserved intellectual-property model. Publication of this repository is not an open-source license or a general invitation to create derivative works.

## Issues
Issues may report defects, interoperability observations, validation failures, or documentation corrections. Do not submit confidential, proprietary, trade-secret, patent-sensitive, or third-party restricted information through public channels.

## Pull Requests
Substantive implementation, ontology, algorithmic, architectural, mathematical, or specification contributions should not be submitted without prior maintainer approval and any required written contributor agreement.

Contributors must submit only material that they have the legal right to submit. Contributions must not introduce incompatible third-party licensing obligations.

## Intellectual Property
Acceptance of substantive outside contributions may require a separate Contributor License Agreement, copyright assignment, invention assignment, or other written agreement. Repository activity alone does not replace such an agreement and does not grant a patent license.

## Technical Requirements
Approved technical contributions must preserve applicable PTM invariants and pass the required validation pipeline, including applicable RDF/Turtle parsing, SHACL validation, algebraic round-trip tests, composition tests, inverse-exponentiation tests, and CI checks.

Passing automated tests does not guarantee acceptance. Semantic correctness, compatibility, security, intellectual-property considerations, and release policy remain separate review requirements.

## AI-Assisted Contributions
Contributors using generative AI or other automated systems remain responsible for provenance, originality, third-party rights, confidentiality, licensing compatibility, and technical correctness.
